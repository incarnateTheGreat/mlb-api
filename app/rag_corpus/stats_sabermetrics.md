# Sabermetrics and Advanced Baseball Statistics

## Introduction

Sabermetrics is the empirical analysis of baseball using statistics. The term comes from the Society for American Baseball Research (SABR). Modern baseball teams rely heavily on sabermetric analysis for player evaluation, strategy, and roster construction.

## Key Sabermetric Metrics

### WAR (Wins Above Replacement)

- Measures a player's contribution in wins compared to a replacement player
- Encompasses batting, baserunning, fielding, and pitching
- Example: A player with 5 WAR contributed about 5 more wins than a replacement player would have
- Generally: 2+ WAR is valuable, 4+ is All-Star caliber, 8+ is elite

### OPS (On-base Plus Slugging)

- Combines On-Base Percentage (OBP) and Slugging Percentage (SLG)
- Measures offensive production efficiency
- Example: .800 OPS is above average, .900+ is excellent

### FIP (Fielding Independent Pitching)

- Measures pitcher skill independent of team defense
- Based on strikes, walks, and home runs only
- Provides clearer picture of pitcher performance than ERA

### Exit Velocity and Launch Angle

- Exit velocity: Speed of ball off batter's bat (measured in mph)
- Launch angle: Angle at which ball leaves bat (measured in degrees)
- Analytics tools use these to predict hit success

## Impact on Modern Baseball

Sabermetrics has transformed player evaluation, game strategy, roster composition, and training methods. Teams now employ data science departments to analyze and apply these metrics competitively.

Source: Society for American Baseball Research (SABR), MLB Advanced Media.
