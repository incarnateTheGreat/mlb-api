# Infield Fly Rule

The infield fly rule applies when there are runners on first and second, or bases loaded, with fewer than two outs.

An infield fly is a fair fly ball (not including a line drive or bunt) that an infielder can catch with ordinary effort.

When the rule is in effect, the batter is out regardless of whether the ball is caught. Runners may advance at their own risk after tagging up if the ball is caught, or immediately if it is not caught.

Source: Official Baseball Rules, Rule 5.09(a)(5).
