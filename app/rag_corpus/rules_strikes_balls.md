# Strike Zone and Strikes Rule

## Definition

The strike zone is the area over home plate from the midpoint between the shoulders and the top of the pants on the high end to the hollow beneath the knee cap on the low end.

A strike is:

1. Any pitch thrown in the strike zone (regardless if swung at) or
2. Any pitch swung at by the batter (whether in the strike zone or not) or
3. When a batter attempts to bunt and the ball is in fair territory
4. When a foul ball is not caught (with exceptions for foul tips and bunts with two strikes)

## Ball

A ball is a pitch delivered to a batter which does not enter the strike zone and at which he does not swing.

## Count

A batter's count refers to the number of balls and strikes. Example: "2-1 count" means 2 balls and 1 strike. After 4 balls, the batter advances to first base (walk). After 3 strikes, the batter is out (strikeout).

Source: Official Baseball Rules, Rule 2.00 and Rule 5.07.
